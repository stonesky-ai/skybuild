# Source and scope

Adapted for SkyBuild from [danyuchn/asd-ste100-skill](https://github.com/danyuchn/asd-ste100-skill), version 0.4.0, source commit `32511c6992ecb5f1971e46a2943f2e6adceedafe`. The upstream MIT license and copyright notice are retained in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

This adaptation preserves the upstream principles of complete grammar, consistent terms, and unchanged facts, modality, conditions and uncertainty. It adds token-conscious compression and SkyBuild authority/evidence safeguards. It does not vendor the upstream linter or reproduce the official ASD dictionary. It is an unofficial STE-inspired writing aid, not a certified ASD-STE100 compliance tool. Consult the [official standard](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html) when formal vocabulary compliance is required.
