## Design priorities

1. Make **deny-before-launch** universal. No launcher calls a model without a fresh, scoped permit that covers desired state, budget, usage freshness, resource reserve, eligible task, dependencies, lease, and capability/version compatibility.
2. Make **stop durable and observable**. Record who stopped what, generation, scope, time, and each launcher's acknowledgement. Independent watchers may detect and report; they cannot override stop.
3. Make **zero-token recovery the default**. Health checks, scanner checks, queue eligibility, lease reconciliation, resource checks, process identity, failure grouping, and bounded log summaries use scripts. Unknown state parks work rather than prompting a model.
4. Make **progress and usage attributable**. Every attempt records parent/launcher, authorization, box, code/instruction versions, phase progress, exit reason, and measured or explicitly estimated usage. Preserve artifact references and incomplete-observation markers.
5. Optimize for **safe useful throughput under explicit authorization**, not maximum busy time. Keep resource reserves. Treat no eligible work as a healthy parked state.
