Circles are task workflow places (phases). Bars are actions (transitions); actors name who or what may perform each action. Blocked and deferred are wait conditions; the journal may retain the underlying phase. Color records journal history, not permission to act. Creation starts in Planned. Edits, dependency or policy changes, and deferral can originate from more than one phase; the diagram shows representative guarded routes. Planning scans, stale or duplicate messages, and rejected actions can add journal evidence without changing phase. This is the proposed workflow from the architecture, not a live workflow engine.
